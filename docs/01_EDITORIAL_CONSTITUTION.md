# Editorial Constitution

## Purpose

This document is intended to hold the non-negotiable editorial principles that govern everything Cynth produces or assists with — the rules that outrank convenience, speed, or any individual feature. It is meant to function as the "constitution" that the article engine, quality gate, and SEO engine are all ultimately answerable to.

## Scope

This document covers editorial principles only: voice, integrity, human authority, and standards of quality. It does not cover technical implementation, prompt wording, or UI design.

## Responsibilities

The Editorial Constitution, once written, should:

- Establish the guiding editorial values for EveryFiveDays content (e.g. accuracy, tone, originality standards).
- Establish the boundary between what Cynth may decide autonomously and what must be escalated to a human editor.
- Establish how author voice and identity are to be respected and protected.
- Serve as the standard that the Quality Gate ([11_QUALITY_GATE.md](11_QUALITY_GATE.md)) checks articles against.

## Current Status

**Not yet defined.** No specific editorial principles, voice guidelines, or standards have been provided for EveryFiveDays. Per project development rules, nothing has been invented or assumed here — this document is a placeholder for content that must come from the editorial owner(s) of the site.

## Future Expansion

Once actual editorial standards are supplied, this document should be expanded to include concrete, checkable rules rather than general statements. It should remain the single source of truth referenced by other engine components.
